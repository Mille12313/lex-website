About

$LEX started as a token inspired by a real Siberian Husky named Lex — not a fictional mascot. Founded by B54 (Bjorn Daems) and launched on Cronos on October 29, 2024, $LEX set out to tackle the trust and sustainability problems common across meme coins with radical transparency, a 0% buy/sell tax, and a deflationary mechanism aiming to cut total supply by over 20% by 2030.

Holders can stake $LEX and OG Passes for rewards today. HuskyWorld is in development (alpha phase), and its planned $SLG utility token is meant to bring in-game burns and real-world buyback-and-burn programs into the mix.

$LEX is not:

  • a pump-and-dump token
  • a moonboy project
  • a generic meme coin
  • an NFT cash grab

It's a pack built for the long haul.
